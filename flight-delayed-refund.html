Flight delayed refund: what you can actually claim

If Google brought you here after a delay or cancel, start here. US rules and EU rules are not the same. Then fill the letter and send it to the airline tonight.

Make my refund letter

United States

If the airline cancelled or significantly delayed or changed the flight and you choose not to travel, you are generally entitled to a refund of the fare (taxes and unused extras included) — not a voucher you didn’t ask for.

DOT’s refund page: transportation.gov — Refunds. A significant change includes leaving 3+ hours early or late on a domestic itinerary, or 6+ hours on an international one.

If you take the delayed flight, a fare refund is usually not owed. You can still ask for expenses the airline promised in its customer service plan.

Bumped (involuntary denied boarding) can mean cash under 14 CFR part 250. Published caps for travel on/after 22 Jan 2025 are $1,075 or $2,150 depending on how late the substitute flight arrives. A US delay is not automatic €600.

EU / EEA / Switzerland

Regulation 261/2004 can mean cash if the flight is covered and the airline cannot prove extraordinary circumstances. Typical published amounts: €250 / €400 / €600 by distance. A delay of 3 hours or more at arrival can qualify. Official: Your Europe — air passenger rights.

Do this tonight

  1. Screenshot the delay/cancel notice and your boarding pass.
  2. Keep receipts if you paid for a hotel, meal, or ride they didn’t provide.
  3. Fill the letter — pick US refund, US bump, or EU 261.
  4. Send it to the operating airline. Do not accept a voucher if you want a refund.
